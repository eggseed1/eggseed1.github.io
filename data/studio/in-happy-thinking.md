my bf read my last blog post and told me that i should probably rethink my negative outlook on the world and myself because it made him feel very sad for me. which i do agree with, although i honestly thought the previous post ended on a relatively positive note? Perhaps i need to rethink what i define as positive

when i used to go to therapy they would make me do these CBT exercises where you try to restructure negative thoughts into more positive ones, which i always had trouble with because i genuinely believed the negative thoughts were simply the realistic ones; that my negative thought was the natural equilibrium of things and optimism was just some delusion. but i've been thinking that even if positive thinking really were a kind of delusion, maybe taking the blue pill is preferable if it means being less miserable and less anxious at times. this is also why for a time last year i tried reading bible verses then praying every day and making myself genuinely believe in them, because i've always regarded religion as perhaps the greatest blue pill ever invented. unfortunately i don't think i'm the sort of person who can just will into becoming religious, so i'll have to settle for the less interesting alternative of rehabilitating my own thoughts.

i've also came to realize that my bad mental states don't actually come out of nowhere, although they always seem to at the time. usually it's something incredibly insignificant, like an unwanted sexual thought that makes me feel somehow dirtied by the mere fact of having it, followed by this enormous guilt and a whole succession of flashing images that reminds me how terrible i am and how i don't deserve to live, until suddenly i've arrived at the conclusion that nothing matters and i should jump. all this over something that would probably mean absolutely nothing to another person. but i've gotten much better at recognizing these thoughts before they completely overwhelm me, and i don't feel any physical impulses to act on them anymore. i really do love being alive and would be terrified to leave the people i love behind. the episodes have gotten shorter too, although they still happen occasionally. i'm happy that i've been getting better in the long run, even if it's difficult to notice sometimes.

anyway, this is a very long-winded way of admitting that my negative thoughts probably aren't some profound insight into the true nature of the world and that i should actually make an effort to change them.
