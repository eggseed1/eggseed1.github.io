original collection notes

If you know me, you know I can’t resist a strange outsider record - and this one’s another gem. Released by a small label called Narrow Way Gospel Line out of Spanish Town, Jamaica, it’s oddly comforting and deeply nostalgic. The lo-fi production and off-key group vocals evoke the scene of a Sunday school class led by a passionate teacher.
