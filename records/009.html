original collection notes

Haven’t posted in a while, but I definitely haven’t stopped collecting records lol

On a trip to Detroit a little while back, I went digging for records, and this one ended up being my favorite find from the whole trip!! The label alone,, Ranger Records already got me (the artist’s name is Forrest Green… on Ranger…). But the record itself is also very charming. Side A is this quirky, upbeat country tune, and Side B flips into a slow, mellow ballad. Forrest Green has such a great smooth voice that I’ll never get tired of. There’s also a female duet on both tracks that I lovee which I’m guessing it’s his wife. It’s very sweet !!

Forrest Green is also a quite accomplished and prominent figure in the country scene, having recorded in the infamous Owen Bradley’s “hut studio” and performing in the grand ole opry shows along side big names. Forrest founded the “Ranger” label himself in 1954, along with his first recording studio. He spent decades producing, arranging, and recording hundreds of songs, not just his own, but also for countless regional artists, plus handful of major names like Warner Mack and Helen Carter. At one point he even had his own TV series and a long-running touring show, The Forrest Green Show.

Anyways hope you all give it a spin it’s quite a great country track ^_^
