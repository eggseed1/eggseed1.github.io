original collection notes

Skip ahead to slide 4 if you want to hear the music!

I’ll admit this record was a bit of an accident (…) I picked it up in a dollar bin thinking it was Blind Blake, the Piedmont blues guitarist from Jacksonville, Florida. Instead, it turned out to be Blind Blake Alphonso Higgs, the Bahamian calypso musician. At first I was embarrassed by the mix-up, but honestly, it’s hard to feel disappointed once you listen. Both “Blakes” are remarkable in their own right, and it’s almost as if the name itself carries a certain musical magic. (At first I figured he must’ve taken his name from the bluesman, but nope, they just happen to share the same name, and both were blind.)

What struck me most was Higgs’ banjo playing - lightning-fast, playful, and irresistibly rhythmic. Even within the distinctly Caribbean flavor of his calypso recordings, you can hear the echoes of American folk and blues traditions. Given the Bahamas’ geographic and cultural closeness to the U.S., that overlap feels natural… Anyways this record is extremely great and can’t recommend it enough!! 😍😍 My favorite track on this is “J P Morgan” and “Jones Oh Jones”
