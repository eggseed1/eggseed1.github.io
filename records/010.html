research / notes

The collector's caption uses “Rockin’ Little Eskimos” and “The Ballad of the Angels”; the documented release spells the titles “Rockin’ Little Eskimo” / “The Ballad of Angel.” Credited to Bobby Swanson and His Sonics on Alaska's Igloo label, no. 1003, issued in November 1959. A contemporary Cash Box review also documents both sides.

original collection notes

One of the best rockabilly single made with one of the best label designs on a 45… I’m sure everyone that loves rockabilly have heard of this record before so there’s not much to say. Though overshadowed by the infamous “Rockin’ Little Eskimos,” side-b “The ballad of the Angels” is also a great pop single… love his voice
