original collection notes

Such a cool little record from Jimmie Dawson!! Hot Tamale Wagon b/w If I Can’t Have You came out around 1957 in Savanna, Illinois on the Pla Boy label and it’s just a great slice of small-town rockabilly. Hot Tamale Wagon is the wild one here, an extremely catchy bopper with this strange, super-repetitive hook that somehow totally works, while the flip If I Can’t Have You slows things down for a straight, heartfelt country ballad. I wonder if Savanna has a significant Hispanic population, which would make sense as to him to writing about tamales 😆

This 45 also shows up on two other tiny local labels, (Iowans and Jimmy Dawson Sings) with the same master, probably just reorders or rebrands for different local markets, which was pretty common with private press records back then.
