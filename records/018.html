original collection notes

Larry Lee Phillipson is perhaps best known for his early rockabilly hit “Bitter Feelings,” recently reissued by the infamous Numero Group. But a decade after that breakout single, Phillipson released his versions of “The Interstate Is Coming Through My Outhouse” and “The Prisoner’s Song” - a noticeable shift in tone and style. These later tracks showcase a more mature voice and a deeper commitment to traditional country music, revealing an artist who had grown significantly since his rockabilly days.

Also to note, Larry Lee Phillipson is a life-long Milwaukee resident. What’s always struck me is how unexpectedly rich Milwaukee’s country music scene is. While cities like Nashville or Austin are typically top of mind when talking about country, Milwaukee has quietly cultivated its own tradition. I’ll be posting more Milwaukee based country 45s…
