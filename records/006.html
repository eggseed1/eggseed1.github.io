original collection notes

Though I haven’t stopped buying records (I still have a whole box of rare 45s I haven’t even logged yet 😅) I’ve been going through some big life changes lately, including moving to a new place and starting a new job, so I haven’t been quite as active here.

Anyhow… this is “Bright Light, Big Star” / “Heart of Gold” by Virgil Weaver on Blue Valley Records. I’m familiar with the Blue Valley label associated with MGM, but this particular imprint seems to be much more obscure. There’s very little information about it online, though it was likely a small local Michigan label, since the record was cut at MCM Studios in Mt. Clemens, Michigan. As far as I can tell, this was the only record ever released by Virgil Weaver, and I haven’t been able to find any other recording credits for its producer, Ron Darlin, either. He had such a great voice though, hopefully there’s some other cut of him out there … If anyone has any info on Blue Valley Records please let me know! Would appreciate it 🙏
