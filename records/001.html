original collection notes

One of my favorite soul records of all time ! I bought this while record hunting in Wisconsin and really came to fall in love with it. Very catchy & fun tune u can listen to anytime of the day. I’m usually not a huge fan of the more refined, soft and sweet 70s soul sound, but this one rlly caught my ears.

This single is by the “Supurbs” (only known recording from them) on the Al-teen record, which was active during the 6-70s in the Chicago ~ Milwaukee soul scene. Couldn’t track down any of the members in the “Supurbs” so I’d appreciate if anyone knows anything of them!
