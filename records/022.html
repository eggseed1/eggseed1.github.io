The blue-and-white label clearly reads JOREL, JR-110B, “VOICE OF ARIZONA,” and RICK STANFORD; the original caption calls the song “Voices of Arizona.” The photographed copy is marked B-side. The A-side and year have not been confirmed from independent sources.

[ search Discogs ][ original Instagram ]

original collection notes

One of my favorite finds today while rummaging through the dollar bins on the South Side of Chicago. Side A is a solid rockabilly cut, but the B-side, “Voices of Arizona,” is a clearrr stand out

It’s a bittersweet country song about trying to “make it big” in Chicago, but coming up short…broke, homesick, and feeling like a disappointment. Pretty ironic as the artist sings about failing to make it, and this record, likely his only one, wounded up forgotten in a bargain bin.

The label, Jorel, only released a few records across an odd mix of genres around Chicagoland. Wonder why this guy decided to do country in Chicago out of all the places.
