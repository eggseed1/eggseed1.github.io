research / notes

The quoted “I belong to the Beat Generation” lyric and Halloween theme identify the song with Bob McFadden & Dor (Rod McKuen). The US Brunswick 9-55140 couples “The Mummy” with “The Beat Generation” (1959). Exact pressing of the photographed copy has not been independently compared to its label.

original collection notes

One of my absolute favorite niches to collect on vinyl is Halloween-inspired, horror-themed music (!!) the stranger, the better. Out of all the spooky novelty records I’ve come across, this one is the one that kicked off my fixation. I loooove the raw character this record have … the eerie atmosphere, tongue-in-cheek charm, and an unbelievably catchy hook that lodges itself in your brain - “i belong to the BEEEEAT GENERATION.” I also love the saxophone at the end-ish of this track.. scratches the right part of my brain ^_^
