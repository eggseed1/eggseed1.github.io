research / notes

The label photograph reads NICKTONE RECORD CO., Lakeland, Florida, CP-6019, SIDE 1, “ALWAYS ALONE,” LEFTY NICKS AND HIS SOUTHERN DRIFTERS. The collector caption provides the flip “Model A Ford Blues” and gives 1961. The CP-6019 printed number may be a matrix rather than a label catalog number.

[ source reference ][ search Discogs ][ original Instagram ]

original collection notes

Released in 1961 in Lakeland, Florida, this is the only record ever put out on the Nicktone label! Side A, “Always Alone,” is straight honky-tonk country and sounds uncannily like Lefty Frizzell (could be just coincidence, or Nicks took after the name from him?). Side B, “Model A Ford Blues,” is the real reason I wanted this record: it’s that perfect mix of “hillbilly” stomp with just enough rock edge to land right in my sweet spot. Also the slightly funky guitar on this side rlly gives it a unique character.
