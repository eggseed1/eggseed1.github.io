A collector's detailed discussion names the B-side as “The Wabash Cannon Ball” and dates the custom disc to 1969, with WJON Broadcasting copyright credit. This matches the train sounds mentioned in the original caption. The exact catalog number has not been confirmed.

original collection notes

Great Appalachian-leaning folk record from the Hill-Dillies. I picked it up originally for Side A, The Eden Valley Fox Hunt, which delivers exactly what you’d hope: lively & playful with a raw, regional charm. But after listening to it at home, Side B really grew on me. I’m a hugeee sucker for train sounds on records and this one nails it.

Assuming it’s a custom press, with the artist name doubling as the label, and one of only two known releases from Minnesota’s WJON Broadcasting Co.
