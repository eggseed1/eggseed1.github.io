research / notes

Buffalo duo Bob & Gene recorded the Marvin Gaye/Tammi Terrell song “If This World Were Mine” on their local Mo Do label. Independent label discographies list the 1971 single Mo Do 125 with “It Won't Go” on the flip. Daptone's artist biography specifically describes their obscure Buffalo 45. Likely this copy; label photo should be checked before treating pressing as certain.

original collection notes

Two guys from Buffalo NY, who never quite made it.
Such a strange cover of the infamous song of Marvin Gaye’s “If This World Was Mine.” Though they’re not the most skilled musicians, there’s an indescribable soul to them that I simply adore.
