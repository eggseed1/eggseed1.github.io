The Woodpecker / Puss on You - Jim & Bill

Cool misprint on the b side which says “Pusan You” instead of “Puss on You.” Took me a while to track down the artist due to this misprint lol
I love collecting novelty records especially ones with animal sounds (my personal fav is wild dogs of kentucky by nervous norvus) and immediately picked this one up after hearing the woodpecker sound on side-a. Very catchy kitschy sorta chill rockabilly record ^_^
