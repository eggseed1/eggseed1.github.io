research / notes

The photographed red Pashlo label shows THE CHRONICLE CHURCH, “MYSTERY,” side 1, PH-1006. A cataloged copy lists the flip as “Bionic Movement” with the paired PH-1007 designation and dates the disc to 1976. The earlier importer label “Chronicles Church” came from the caption; the physical label says “Chronicle Church.”

original collection notes

Released in 1976 by “The Chronicles Church” (guess it… a gospel group) on Pashlo Records out of Oakland, CA. Such a perfect record to wind down to. I love picking up local labels whenever I travel, and this one was such a great souvenir from my three-month stay in San Francisco this summer. It just feels like a norcal record haha
